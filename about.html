About Gaaba Sport

A Sialkot-based boxing and MMA equipment manufacturer helping international buyers build dependable combat sports product lines.

Our Story

Manufacturing heritage with a global mindset.

Sialkot, Pakistan is known worldwide for skilled sports goods craftsmanship. Gaaba Sport carries that heritage into modern combat sports manufacturing with export-ready boxing gloves, MMA gloves, and training gear.

We support B2B wholesale buyers, gyms, retailers, distributors, and private label brands that need consistent quality, competitive pricing, flexible customization, and responsive communication.

  • OEM/private label manufacturing
  • Buyer-focused sampling and production workflow
  • Quality checks for stitching, padding, finish, and packing
  • Export-friendly packaging and documentation support
